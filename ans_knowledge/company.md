## What ANS sells

ANS's core proposition for this tool is two product families:

1. **Network emulators** (Linktropy and Netropy series) — WAN condition simulation in the lab
2. **Traffic generators** (Netropy TrafficGen suite) — application and attack traffic generation in the lab

See `products/emulators.md` and `products/traffic_generators.md` for full product detail.

## Who ANS sells to

Target profile: enterprise organisations with ≥1000 staff that have in-house network infrastructure teams and run their own labs for testing. Key verticals:

- **Financial services** — regulated under DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act); network resilience testing is a compliance requirement
- **Telecoms and carriers** — equipment testing and benchmarking
- **Defence and government** — satellite communication testing, mission-critical systems (reference: Lockheed Martin THAAD)
- **Healthcare** — NHS and private hospital networks, HIPAA/UK DCB0129 compliance
- **Retail and e-commerce** — application performance under peak load (Black Friday, high-traffic events)
- **Manufacturing / industrial** — operational technology (OT) network testing, factory automation
- **Technology vendors and system integrators** — product development and QA

## Regulatory tailwinds (for opportunity framing)

- **DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act):** Financial entities in the EU/UK must demonstrate ICT resilience testing. Network emulators are a natural fit for resilience testing documentation.
- **NIS2 Directive:** Expanded to cover more sectors; network testing helps evidence security controls.
- **UK Cyber Essentials / NCSC frameworks:** Network performance and security validation increasingly expected.
- **PCI-DSS v4:** Tighter requirements for network segmentation testing.
- **FedRAMP / HIPAA:** US-operating UK multinationals face these requirements.
